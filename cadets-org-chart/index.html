Cadets Org Chart - Proof & Build Guide

Replacing the manually-maintained Visio branch org chart (108 posts, 10 departments, 5 vacancies) with a Microsoft-only, SharePoint-list-driven Power Apps chart. Phases 0-2 are confirmed passed — the Image-control-renders-generated-SVG approach genuinely works, including at real department scale. Current stage: combining all 10 departments into one full-sheet render, still on local data, before any SharePoint schema or editing screens are built.

What This Page Is

Working proof log for the Cadets Org Chart canvas app (local project: C:\Users\Phaderon\Projects\PowerApps-Apps\cadets-org-chart — no separate GitHub/screen-YAML repo yet, this project hasn't reached real screens). Full brief, the four-page mockup PDF and the separate interactive HTML concept live in that project's docs/handoff/ folder.

Why this starts with a proof, not a build: the Power Apps Bible has zero prior lab-verified experience with a computed SVG in an Image control, or with Print()/A3 export — this is genuinely new ground for this app family, not a known-working pattern being reapplied. Phase 0 below is the cheapest possible test of the riskiest assumption.
