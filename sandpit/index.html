Sandpit

A design-only testing ground. Screens here are read-only look-and-feel passes — pre-filled fields, no SharePoint lists, no data wiring — pasted into a throwaway Studio app purely to validate visual design before it goes anywhere near a real build. The intent is to work out a design language once here and reuse it across new apps (Training Tracker, Org Chart, and others), rather than re-deriving it each time.
